# Results of the Four-Week Self-Checkout Pilot, Elm Street Store

## Summary

From September 28 to October 25, the Elm Street store ran four self-checkout lanes alongside its regular registers. Average wait times at peak hours fell from 6.5 minutes to 3.8 minutes, and 31% of transactions went through self-checkout by the final week. Shrinkage at the self-checkout lanes was higher than at staffed registers, but within the range we planned for. We recommend extending self-checkout to two more stores in Q1, with one change to staffing.

## What We Tested

- Four self-checkout lanes replaced two staffed registers.
- One staff member was assigned to help at the self-checkout area during opening hours.
- Customers could choose either type of lane.

## Results

| Measure | Before pilot | Pilot (week 4) |
|---|---|---|
| Average peak-hour wait | 6.5 min | 3.8 min |
| Share of transactions at self-checkout | – | 31% |
| Customer satisfaction (survey, 1–5) | 3.9 | 4.1 |
| Shrinkage at self-checkout lanes | – | 1.4% of sales |
| Shrinkage at staffed registers | 0.6% | 0.6% |

**Usage grew each week**: 14% in week 1, 22% in week 2, 27% in week 3 and 31% in week 4.

**Customers over 65 rarely used it**: they made up 24% of the store's customers but 6% of self-checkout transactions. Several told staff they preferred a person.

**Most problems were with weighing produce**: about half of the help requests at the self-checkout area involved loose fruit and vegetables.

## Issues

- One staff member was not enough during the Saturday morning rush. Lines formed at self-checkout while customers waited for help with produce or age-restricted items.
- Shrinkage at self-checkout (1.4%) was more than double the staffed rate, though below the 2% limit we set at the start.

## Recommendation

1. Roll out self-checkout to the Oak Avenue and Riverside stores in Q1 2027.
2. Schedule a second helper at self-checkout on Saturday and Sunday mornings.
3. Keep at least three staffed registers open at all times, so customers who prefer one can still use it.
4. Review shrinkage monthly. If it goes above 2% for two months in a row, add a spot-check at the exit.
