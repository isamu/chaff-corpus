# Trip Report: Regional Libraries Conference, Portland

Dates: October 14–16, 2026
Attended by: Maria Gonzalez, Adult Services

I went to this year's conference mainly to learn how other libraries are handling the rise in requests for digital help: people coming in to get help with phones, online forms and government websites. That's now a big part of what our front desk does, and we've been working it out as we go.

Here's what I came back with.

## Sessions worth mentioning

**"Tech help without burning out staff"** was the most useful session for us. The Spokane library system described how they moved from drop-in help to booked 30-minute appointments. Staff said the change made the work less stressful, because they could prepare and weren't juggling three people at once. Visitors didn't seem to mind booking, as long as there was still a short walk-in slot each day. We could try something similar without much cost.

**"Volunteers in digital literacy"** covered training retired volunteers to run small group sessions. The speakers were honest that it takes a lot of upfront training and that some volunteers don't stay long. Still, two libraries said it doubled the number of people they could help each week.

The session on **AI tools for cataloguing** was interesting, but it's not something we're ready for. I'd skip it if we send someone next year.

## Conversations

I spent a good part of the second day talking with staff from smaller libraries about our size. Two of them have offered to share their appointment booking forms and the handouts they give people (for example, a one-page guide to resetting passwords). I'll circulate those when they arrive.

## What I'd suggest we try

1. A three-month trial of booked tech help appointments, two afternoons a week, while keeping a 30-minute walk-in slot.
2. Talking to the Friends of the Library group about whether any of their members would want to volunteer.

I can put together a short plan for the first idea before the December staff meeting.

## Costs

Registration $320, hotel (2 nights) $410, mileage $96. Total: $826, within the training budget.
