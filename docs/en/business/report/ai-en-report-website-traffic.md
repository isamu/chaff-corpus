# Website Performance, Q3 2026: Board Summary

## The short version

Visits rose 18% over Q2, but online sales fell 4%. More people are finding us; fewer of them are buying. The drop is concentrated on mobile, where checkout completion fell after the July site update. We recommend a focused fix to mobile checkout in Q4, at an estimated cost of $40,000.

## Key numbers

| Metric | Q2 | Q3 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website visits | 412,000 | 486,000 | +18% |
| Online orders | 9,850 | 9,460 | −4% |
| Conversion rate | 2.4% | 1.9% | −0.5 pts |
| Mobile share of visits | 61% | 66% | +5 pts |
| Mobile checkout completion | 48% | 37% | −11 pts |

## What happened

The increase in visits came mainly from the autumn social media campaign, which brought in 52,000 new visitors, most of them on phones.

The July update added a required account-creation step at checkout. On desktop, completion barely moved. On mobile, it fell by 11 points, and customer feedback points to the same cause: the form is long and hard to fill in on a small screen.

## Recommendation

1. Allow guest checkout on mobile again (low cost; can be done in November).
2. Shorten the mobile checkout form from 14 fields to 7.
3. Track mobile completion weekly and report back to the board in January.

Estimated cost for items 1 and 2: $40,000, within the existing digital budget. If mobile completion returns to its Q2 level, we estimate it would recover about $210,000 in sales per quarter.

## What we are not recommending

We considered pausing the social media campaign until checkout is fixed. We don't recommend it: the campaign is bringing in new customers at a low cost, and the problem is what happens after they arrive, not how they arrive.
