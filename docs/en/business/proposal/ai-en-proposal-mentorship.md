# Proposal: Internal Mentorship Program

## Executive Summary

This proposal recommends launching a structured mentorship program that pairs employees in their first two years with experienced colleagues from other teams. The program would run in six-month cycles, starting with a pilot of 20 pairs in March 2027. The estimated cost is $12,000 per year, mainly for training and program materials.

## Background

Over the past year, 38% of employees who left the company had been with us for less than two years. In exit interviews, the most common reasons were a lack of career direction and feeling disconnected from the wider organization. At the same time, our engagement survey shows that employees who say they have "someone at work who supports my development" score 22 points higher on overall satisfaction.

## Proposed Program

**Structure**

- Mentees: employees in their first two years, who apply voluntarily
- Mentors: employees with at least five years of experience, who volunteer and are matched with a mentee from a different team
- Duration: six months per cycle
- Commitment: one 45-minute meeting every two weeks

**Support for participants**

- A two-hour training session for mentors on giving feedback and setting goals
- A short guide for mentees on how to get the most out of the relationship
- A mid-point check-in with HR to address any issues with the pairing

## Expected Benefits

- Improved retention of early-career employees
- Stronger connections across teams
- Development opportunities for senior employees who want to build leadership skills
- Earlier identification of employees with high potential

## Costs

| Item | Annual cost |
|---|---|
| Mentor training (external facilitator, 2 sessions) | $6,000 |
| Program materials and platform | $4,000 |
| Kick-off and closing events | $2,000 |
| **Total** | **$12,000** |

Participants' time is the largest indirect cost: about 20 hours per person over six months.

## Measuring Success

We will compare pilot participants with non-participants on:

- 12-month retention
- Engagement survey scores
- Participant feedback at the end of each cycle

## Timeline

- December 2026: Approve proposal
- January 2027: Recruit mentors and mentees
- February 2027: Matching and mentor training
- March 2027: Pilot cycle begins
- September 2027: Evaluate pilot and decide on expansion

## Recommendation

We recommend approving the pilot program for 2027. A small investment now could reduce the cost of early-career turnover and help build a more connected organization.
