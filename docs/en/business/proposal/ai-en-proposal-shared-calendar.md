# Can we please have one shared team calendar?

Hi team,

Here's a small idea that I think would save us all some headaches.

## The problem

Right now, figuring out who's around on any given day means checking three places: the vacation spreadsheet, the #out-of-office channel, and whatever people put in their own calendars. Last month we scheduled a client demo for a day when two of the three people presenting were on leave. We caught it, but only two days before.

## The idea

One shared Google Calendar called "Team – Who's Out." Anyone who's off, at a conference, or working odd hours adds an event. That's it.

- Vacations and sick days: all-day event with your name
- Conferences or travel: same thing, with where you are
- Half days: just block the hours

You can overlay it on your own calendar, so you'd see it when you're scheduling things anyway.

## What happens to the spreadsheet?

HR still needs the vacation spreadsheet for leave tracking, so that stays. The calendar is just for the rest of us to see at a glance who's around. Yes, it means entering your time off in two places. I think thirty seconds of double entry is worth not double-booking a client.

## Trying it out

I'll set the calendar up this week and share it with everyone. Let's give it until the end of the year. If nobody's using it by then, we drop it, no hard feelings.
