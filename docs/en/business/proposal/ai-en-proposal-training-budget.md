# Proposal: A Personal Training Budget for the Support Team

**From:** Grace Okafor, Customer Support Lead
**To:** Department heads
**Date:** November 12, 2026

## What I'm asking for

$500 per person per year for each of the 14 people on the support team, to spend on training they choose themselves, as long as it relates to their work. Total: $7,000 a year.

## Why

Our support team handles questions about billing, integrations and data exports, and the product changes every month. Right now, people learn new features from release notes and from each other. That works, but slowly. In the last quarter, 18% of tickets were escalated to engineering, and when we reviewed a sample of 50 of them, about a third could have been answered by the support agent with a bit more technical knowledge.

There is also a retention side. Two of the three people who left the team this year told me in their exit conversations that they didn't see a way to grow in the role.

## What people could spend it on

Some examples, based on what the team has asked about:

- An online SQL course, so agents can look into data questions themselves
- A certification for the integration platform most of our customers use
- A writing course, for help articles and difficult customer emails
- Tickets to a regional customer support conference

They'd agree on the choice with me first, and anything outside the obvious would go to the department head.

## What it won't cover

Software subscriptions, hardware, or courses unrelated to the job.

## How we'd know it's working

After a year I'll report on three things: the escalation rate, how many people used the budget and on what, and whether anyone who used it left the team. I'm not promising a specific drop in escalations, because product changes affect that number too, but I expect it to move.

## Cost in context

$7,000 is less than the recruiting fee for replacing one support agent, which was $9,400 for the last hire.
