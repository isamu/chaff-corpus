# Proposal: Replacing the Monthly Newsletter with a Weekly Update

## Summary

We propose replacing the monthly staff newsletter with a short weekly email update, starting in January 2027.

## The current situation

The monthly newsletter is currently around 12 pages long and takes the communications team about four days to put together each month. However, analytics show that only about 20% of staff open it, and most of those who do spend less than a minute on it.

Much of its content is also out of date by the time it is published. For example, announcements of events that have already taken place, or project updates that teams have already shared through other channels.

## What we propose

A weekly email, sent every Monday morning, containing:

- Three to five short items, each of one or two sentences
- A link to more detail for anyone who wants it
- A brief "this week" section listing deadlines and events

The aim is for each update to take no more than two minutes to read.

## Why this would work better

- **More timely:** news would reach staff within a week, rather than up to a month later.
- **More likely to be read:** shorter emails are more likely to be opened and read in full.
- **Less work:** we estimate a weekly update would take about half a day to prepare, or two days a month in total, half of the current effort.

## Trial period

We suggest running the weekly update for three months, from January to March 2027. At the end of the trial, we will compare open rates with those of the current newsletter and run a short staff survey before deciding whether to continue.

We would welcome any feedback on this proposal.
