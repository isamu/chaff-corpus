# Minutes of the Board of Directors Meeting

**Riverside Community Food Bank**
**Date:** Tuesday, November 10, 2026
**Time:** 6:30 p.m.
**Location:** Riverside Community Center, Room B

**Present:** Margaret Ellis (Chair), David Kim (Treasurer), Susan Patel (Secretary), Robert Hughes, Angela Martinez, James O'Brien
**Absent:** Linda Fowler (excused)
**Also present:** Carla Nguyen, Executive Director

## 1. Call to Order

The Chair called the meeting to order at 6:32 p.m. A quorum was present.

## 2. Approval of Minutes

The minutes of the October 13, 2026 meeting were reviewed.
**Motion:** To approve the minutes as presented. Moved by R. Hughes, seconded by A. Martinez. **Carried unanimously.**

## 3. Treasurer's Report

D. Kim presented the financial report for the period ending October 31, 2026. Total revenue year to date is $412,600, which is 7% above budget, mainly due to a larger than expected grant from the county. Expenses are $388,900, 3% above budget, primarily because of increased food purchasing costs.
**Motion:** To accept the Treasurer's report. Moved by J. O'Brien, seconded by S. Patel. **Carried unanimously.**

## 4. Executive Director's Report

C. Nguyen reported that the food bank served 2,140 households in October, an increase of 18% over October 2025. She noted that demand is expected to rise further in December. The refrigerated truck purchased in August is in full use and has allowed the food bank to accept two new grocery store partners.

## 5. Old Business

**5.1 Volunteer coordinator position.** C. Nguyen reported that the position was filled on November 2. The new coordinator, Michael Brooks, will attend the December meeting.

## 6. New Business

**6.1 Holiday food drive.** The board discussed the plan for the annual holiday food drive, December 1–20. A. Martinez raised concerns about volunteer capacity on weekends. C. Nguyen will work with the new volunteer coordinator to set up a weekend schedule.

**6.2 Proposal to extend Saturday hours.** C. Nguyen proposed extending Saturday distribution hours from 9 a.m.–12 p.m. to 9 a.m.–2 p.m., starting in January, at an estimated additional cost of $4,800 per year for staffing.
**Motion:** To approve extended Saturday hours beginning January 2027. Moved by D. Kim, seconded by R. Hughes. **Carried (5 in favor, 1 opposed).** J. O'Brien opposed, citing the budget overrun in food purchasing.

## 7. Next Meeting

Tuesday, December 8, 2026, at 6:30 p.m., Riverside Community Center, Room B.

## 8. Adjournment

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 7:48 p.m.

Respectfully submitted,
Susan Patel, Secretary
