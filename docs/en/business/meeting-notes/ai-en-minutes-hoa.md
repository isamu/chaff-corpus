# Maple Grove HOA – Meeting Notes, October 29

About 25 residents came to this month's meeting at the clubhouse, plus four of the five board members (Greg was away). Janet chaired and I took notes.

## Pool resurfacing

This was the main topic and took most of the hour. The pool surface is cracking in several places, and the contractor who inspected it in September said it should be redone before next summer.

We have two quotes: $38,000 from Blue Wave Pools and $44,500 from Sunset Pool Services. Sunset's quote includes new tile along the waterline; Blue Wave's doesn't. The reserve fund has about $52,000, so either one is affordable without a special assessment, but it would leave the fund thin.

A few residents asked whether we could put this off another year. Janet said the contractor's view was that the cracks will get worse over the winter, and a delay could mean more expensive repairs. Several people also pointed out that the pool is one of the main things people use the HOA for.

The board will vote on which quote to accept at the November meeting. Before then, Mark will ask Blue Wave what adding the tile would cost, so we can compare the two quotes fairly.

## Parking on Birch Lane

Residents on Birch Lane say cars are being parked overnight along the curve near the mailboxes, which makes it hard for the garbage truck to get through. The board agreed to put up two "No Overnight Parking" signs on that stretch and to send a reminder in the next newsletter. Linda will order the signs.

## Halloween

Quick reminder that trick-or-treating is 5:30 to 8:00 on Saturday. The clubhouse will be open for the kids' costume parade at 5:00.
