# Tallyroot Raises $12 Million Series A to Help Small Restaurants Cut Food Waste

*Funding will expand Tallyroot's inventory forecasting platform to 2,000 new restaurants and grow its engineering team*

**AUSTIN, Texas — November 24, 2026** — Tallyroot, the inventory forecasting platform built for independent restaurants, today announced it has raised $12 million in Series A funding. The round was led by Harbor Lane Ventures, with participation from existing investors Mesa Seed Partners and Copperline Capital.

Independent restaurants throw away an estimated 4% to 10% of the food they buy, often because ordering is based on gut feel rather than data. Tallyroot connects to a restaurant's point-of-sale system and uses past sales, weather, local events, and seasonal trends to forecast how much of each ingredient the kitchen will need. Owners and chefs get a suggested order list each morning that they can adjust and send to suppliers in a few taps.

Since launching in 2024, Tallyroot has grown to more than 600 restaurants across Texas, Colorado, and Arizona. Customers report reducing food waste by an average of 23% within their first three months on the platform.

"Big restaurant chains have whole teams forecasting demand. The family-owned taqueria down the street has the owner, a notebook, and a lot of guesswork," said Priya Natarajan, co-founder and CEO of Tallyroot. "We started Tallyroot to give independent restaurants the same kind of tools, in a form they can actually use during a busy service. This funding lets us bring that to a lot more kitchens."

Tallyroot will use the new funding to:

- Expand to new markets in the Southeast and Pacific Northwest, with a goal of reaching 2,000 additional restaurants by the end of 2027
- Double the size of its engineering and data science team
- Build direct integrations with more regional food distributors
- Launch a Spanish-language version of the app

"Food costs are the biggest controllable expense for most restaurants, and margins are thin," said Marcus Webb, partner at Harbor Lane Ventures, who will join Tallyroot's board of directors. "Tallyroot has found a way to make forecasting practical for operators who don't have time for spreadsheets. The results their customers are seeing speak for themselves."

As part of the announcement, Tallyroot is also introducing Waste Log, a new feature that lets kitchen staff record discarded food by scanning a barcode or tapping a photo. The data feeds back into Tallyroot's forecasts so they get more accurate over time.

### About Tallyroot

Tallyroot helps independent restaurants order the right amount of food. Its forecasting platform connects to popular point-of-sale systems and turns sales history into daily ordering recommendations, reducing waste and saving time. Founded in 2024, Tallyroot is headquartered in Austin, Texas. Learn more at tallyroot.example.

### About Harbor Lane Ventures

Harbor Lane Ventures is an early-stage venture capital firm investing in software for the food, hospitality, and retail industries.
