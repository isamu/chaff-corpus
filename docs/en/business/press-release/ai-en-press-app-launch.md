# GreenLeaf Launches Free App to Help Home Gardeners Grow More with Less Water

**PORTLAND, Ore., November 17, 2026** — GreenLeaf, a gardening technology company, today announced the launch of the GreenLeaf app, a free mobile app that helps home gardeners know exactly when and how much to water their plants.

Overwatering is one of the most common mistakes home gardeners make, wasting water and damaging plant roots. The GreenLeaf app combines local weather forecasts, soil type and plant-specific needs to create a personalized watering schedule for each garden.

"Most people water on a fixed schedule, whether it rained yesterday or not," said Dana Reyes, co-founder and CEO of GreenLeaf. "We wanted to give gardeners a simple way to water based on what their plants actually need. In our testing, users cut their outdoor water use by an average of 30% without any loss in plant health."

**Key features of the GreenLeaf app include:**

- **Personalized watering schedules** based on plant type, soil and local weather
- **Rain alerts** that automatically adjust the schedule after rainfall
- **A plant library** with care guides for more than 1,500 common garden plants
- **Garden journal** for tracking plantings, harvests and notes throughout the season

The app was developed with input from master gardeners and tested by more than 2,000 home gardeners across the Pacific Northwest during the 2026 growing season.

The GreenLeaf app is available now as a free download on the App Store and Google Play. An optional premium subscription, GreenLeaf Plus, offers additional features such as smart sprinkler integration and pest identification for $4.99 per month.

**About GreenLeaf**

Founded in 2024 and based in Portland, Oregon, GreenLeaf builds simple tools that help people garden more sustainably. The company's mission is to make water-wise gardening easy for everyone. Learn more at www.greenleafapp.example.
