# Brightnest Voluntarily Recalls SnugSleep Baby Monitor Charging Cables Due to Overheating Risk

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**COLUMBUS, Ohio — November 24, 2026** — Brightnest Home Products, Inc. is voluntarily recalling the USB-C charging cables supplied with its SnugSleep Video Baby Monitor, model SN-200, because the cables can overheat at the connector during charging, posing a burn and fire hazard.

The recall affects approximately 48,000 cables sold in the United States and Canada. The baby monitor units themselves are not affected by this recall.

### Recalled Product

The recall involves the white, 1.5-meter USB-C charging cable included in the box with the SnugSleep Video Baby Monitor, model SN-200, sold between March 1, 2026, and October 15, 2026. The affected cables have the date code **2602**, **2603**, or **2604** printed on the connector housing at the parent-unit end. Cables with other date codes, and cables sold separately, are not included in this recall.

The SnugSleep SN-200 was sold at major retailers nationwide and online for about $129.

### Incidents

Brightnest has received six reports of the cable connector becoming hot to the touch, including two reports of minor damage to the parent unit's charging port. No injuries have been reported.

### What Consumers Should Do

Consumers should **immediately stop using** the recalled cables and unplug them. The baby monitor can continue to be used with any other USB-C cable and power adapter rated at 5V/2A or higher.

Brightnest will send a free replacement cable to all affected customers. To request a replacement, consumers can:

- Visit www.brightnest.example/recall
- Call Brightnest toll-free at (555) 019-2640, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. ET, and Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET

Consumers will be asked for the date code on the cable and a mailing address. Brightnest will include a prepaid envelope for returning the recalled cable.

"The safety of families who use our products is our top priority," said Thomas Reilly, President of Brightnest Home Products. "We are taking this step out of an abundance of caution, and we apologize to our customers for the inconvenience."

This recall is being conducted in cooperation with the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission and Health Canada.

### About Brightnest Home Products

Brightnest Home Products, Inc., based in Columbus, Ohio, designs baby and nursery products, including video monitors, sound machines and night lights.
