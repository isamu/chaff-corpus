# Lakeshore Maritime Museum Opens "Below the Ice," an Exhibition on Winter Shipwrecks of the Great Lakes

**DULUTH, Minn., November 24, 2026** — The Lakeshore Maritime Museum will open *Below the Ice: Winter Shipwrecks of the Great Lakes* on Saturday, January 16, 2027. The exhibition runs through May 31, 2027, in the museum's main gallery.

*Below the Ice* tells the stories of ships lost on Lake Superior during the late-season shipping months of November and December, when storms on the lake are at their worst. At its center are objects recovered from the *Edwin Harlow*, a wooden steamer that sank near the Apostle Islands in 1905 and was rediscovered by volunteer divers in 2023. Many of the items, including the ship's bell, a crew member's pocket watch, and a set of cargo tags, are being shown to the public for the first time.

The exhibition also includes:

- Underwater footage and 3D scans of four wreck sites, filmed by the volunteer dive team
- Letters and newspaper reports from families waiting for news of the crews
- A hands-on area where visitors can try reading weather signals and a ship's chart the way captains did in 1905
- A section on how winter shipping changed after the storms of the early 1900s, including new weather warning systems

"These ships are part of the history of almost every town on this shore," said Helen Kowalski, curator of the exhibition. "When the divers brought up the bell from the *Harlow*, we knew we had to find a way to put it in front of people and tell the crew's story properly."

The museum worked with descendants of the *Harlow*'s crew, two of whom will speak at the opening.

### Opening Day

The exhibition opens to the public at 10 a.m. on Saturday, January 16. Admission is free that day. At 1 p.m., members of the dive team will give a talk on how they found and documented the wreck. Seating for the talk is limited and first come, first served.

### Visiting

The museum is open Tuesday through Sunday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Admission is $12 for adults, $8 for seniors and students, and free for children under 12 and museum members. *Below the Ice* is included in general admission.

Group and school visits can be booked through the museum's education office.

### About the Lakeshore Maritime Museum

The Lakeshore Maritime Museum preserves and shares the history of shipping, fishing, and lighthouse life on Lake Superior. The museum is a nonprofit organization supported by members, donors, and grants.
