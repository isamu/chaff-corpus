# Hillside Public Library and Northgate Community College to Offer Free Computer Classes

**HILLSIDE, Ohio, December 1, 2026** — Starting in January, Hillside residents will be able to take free evening computer classes at the Hillside Public Library, taught by instructors from Northgate Community College.

The classes cover basic skills: setting up an email account, using a word processor, filling in online forms, and spotting scam emails and texts. Each course runs for four weeks, with one 90-minute class a week. There are 12 places in each course, and laptops are provided.

The partnership grew out of a problem both organizations saw. Library staff were helping more people every month with tasks like job applications and benefits forms, often one person at a time at the front desk. The college, meanwhile, had instructors and a curriculum but no location on the east side of the city, where many of the people who need the classes live.

"People were coming to us because they had nowhere else to ask," said Ruth Alvarez, director of the Hillside Public Library. "Now we can offer them something better than five minutes at the desk."

Mark Delaney, dean of continuing education at Northgate Community College, said the college would cover instructor costs for the first year through a state workforce grant.

The first courses start on January 12 and January 14, 2027. Registration opens December 8 at the library's front desk, by phone at (555) 410-2233, or online at hillsidelibrary.example/classes. No college enrollment is required.
