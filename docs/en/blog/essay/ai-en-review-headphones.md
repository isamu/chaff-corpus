# Noise-Cancelling Headphones Review: Six Months of Commuting, Flights and Open-Plan Offices

I bought a pair of over-ear noise-cancelling headphones in the spring for about $280, mostly for my train commute. After six months of daily use, here's my honest take on what they get right and where they fall short.

## The Short Version

**Pros:**
- Excellent noise cancelling on trains and planes
- Comfortable for several hours at a time
- Battery life easily lasts a week of commuting

**Cons:**
- Noise cancelling is weaker against voices
- The touch controls are too sensitive
- Call quality is average outdoors

## Noise Cancelling

This is the main reason to buy them, and it doesn't disappoint. The low rumble of the train almost disappears the moment you turn noise cancelling on. On a five-hour flight in June, the engine noise faded to a soft hum, and I arrived far less tired than usual.

Where it struggles is with voices. In my open-plan office, I can still hear nearby conversations, just quieter. That's true of most noise-cancelling headphones, but it's worth knowing if office chatter is your main problem.

## Comfort

The ear cushions are soft and deep enough that my ears don't touch the inside. I can wear them for three or four hours before I feel the need to take them off. They do get warm, though. On a hot day in August, I found myself taking them off between trains.

## Sound Quality

Out of the box, the sound leans toward bass. It's fun for pop and electronic music but a little muddy for podcasts and acoustic tracks. Thankfully, the companion app has an equalizer, and after turning down the bass a couple of notches, the sound became clear and balanced.

## Battery Life

The manufacturer claims 30 hours with noise cancelling on, and that matches my experience. I charge them about once a week. A 10-minute charge gives several hours of playback, which has saved me more than once.

## Controls and Calls

The touch panel on the right ear cup lets you swipe to change the volume and tap to pause. In practice, it's too sensitive. I've paused my music many times just by adjusting the headphones or pulling up my hood.

For calls, the microphones work well indoors. Outside, especially on windy days, people told me I sounded distant.

## Verdict

If you commute by train or fly often, these headphones are easy to recommend. The noise cancelling, comfort and battery life are all excellent for the price. If you mainly want to block out conversations in an office, or you take a lot of calls outdoors, you may want to try them in a store first.

**Rating: 4 out of 5**
