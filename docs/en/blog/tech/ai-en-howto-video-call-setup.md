# Look and sound better on video calls with what you already have

You don't need to buy a ring light or a new microphone to look and sound decent on video calls. Most of the improvement comes from where you sit and how you set up your laptop. Here is what to check, starting with what makes the biggest difference.

## Sound first

People will put up with a grainy picture, but they stop listening when the sound is bad.

**Use headphones with a microphone.** The earbuds that came with your phone are better than your laptop's built-in microphone, because the microphone is closer to your mouth and it doesn't pick up the sound from your speakers. This one change fixes most echo problems.

**Pick the quietest room you can.** Rooms with carpet, curtains and furniture sound better than kitchens and bare rooms, which make your voice bounce around.

**Mute when you're not talking** in meetings with more than a few people.

## Light

**Face the window, don't sit with your back to it.** With a window behind you, the camera adjusts to the bright background and your face ends up dark. If you can't move your desk, close the curtains behind you.

**In the evening, put a lamp behind your laptop**, pointing toward you. A bit off to one side looks more natural than straight on.

Overhead lights alone tend to put shadows under your eyes, so add a lamp if those are all you have.

## Camera position

**Raise the laptop so the camera is at eye level.** A stack of books works. When the camera is below your face, people are looking up your nose.

**Sit about an arm's length away**, so the frame shows your head and shoulders, with a little space above your head.

**Look at the camera when you're making an important point.** It's awkward, because you can't see people's reactions while you do it, but to them it looks like eye contact.

## Background

You don't need a perfect background. A plain wall or a tidy bookshelf works. Check what's in the frame before an important call. Laundry and unmade beds are the usual problems.

Virtual backgrounds are fine, but they tend to cut off your hands and hair when you move. A blurred background usually looks better.

## Do a test before important calls

Most video apps have a preview or a test call. Open it five minutes before an interview or a client meeting and check the sound, the light and the frame. Or call a friend and ask how you look and sound.
