# Riverbend Community Pool: Frequently Asked Questions

## When is the pool open?

The pool is open from Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day.

- Monday to Friday: 11 a.m. to 7 p.m.
- Saturday and Sunday: 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.
- Lap swim only: Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 6:30 a.m. to 8:30 a.m.

The pool closes for 15 minutes at the top of every other hour (noon, 2 p.m., 4 p.m. and 6 p.m.) for a safety break.

## How much does it cost?

| | Riverbend residents | Non-residents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily admission (age 3 and up) | $5 | $8 |
| Season pass, individual | $60 | $95 |
| Season pass, household (up to 5 people) | $150 | $240 |

Children under 3 get in free. Seniors 65 and over pay half the daily price.

## How do I buy a season pass?

Season passes are sold at the Parks and Recreation office in City Hall and at the pool's front gate. You'll need a photo ID and, for the resident rate, proof of address such as a utility bill. Household passes need a photo of each member, which we can take when you buy the pass.

## Do children need an adult with them?

Children under 10 must have an adult (16 or older) with them in the pool area at all times. Children under 6, and anyone who can't swim, must have an adult within arm's reach in the water.

Kids 10 and older can come on their own once they pass a short swim test with a lifeguard: one length of the pool without stopping, and treading water for one minute. Those who pass get a wristband for the season.

## What should I bring?

A towel, sunscreen, and a lock if you want to use a locker. Swim diapers are required for children who are not toilet trained. Glass containers are not allowed anywhere in the pool area.

## Can I bring food?

Yes, you can eat in the picnic area next to the snack bar. Food and drinks (other than water in a plastic bottle) are not allowed on the pool deck.

## What happens if there's a storm?

If we hear thunder or see lightning, everyone has to leave the water. We reopen 30 minutes after the last thunder. If the pool stays closed for more than an hour, you can ask at the gate for a rain check for that day's admission.

## Can I have a birthday party at the pool?

Yes. The picnic shelter can be reserved on Saturdays and Sundays for two-hour parties of up to 25 people. The fee is $75 for residents and $110 for non-residents, plus admission for each guest. Book at least two weeks ahead through the Parks and Recreation office.

## Do you offer swim lessons?

Yes. Group lessons for ages 4 to 12 run in two-week sessions in June and July. Registration opens May 1. Private lessons can be arranged with our certified instructors at the front gate.

Call the Parks and Recreation office at (555) 284-1190, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. During pool hours, you can also call the pool office at (555) 284-1195.
