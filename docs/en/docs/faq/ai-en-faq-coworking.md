# Frequently Asked Questions

## Membership

### What membership options do you offer?

We offer three membership types:

- **Day Pass** – Access to any open desk for a single day, from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.
- **Flex Membership** – 10 days of access per month, with any open desk available on the days you come in.
- **Dedicated Desk** – Your own desk, available 24/7, with a lockable drawer and storage.

All memberships include Wi-Fi, printing credits, coffee and tea, and access to shared common areas.

### Can I try the space before signing up?

Yes. Your first day pass is free. Just book a tour through our website or stop by the front desk during staffed hours and we'll get you set up.

### Is there a minimum commitment?

No. Flex and Dedicated Desk memberships are billed monthly and you can cancel at any time with 30 days' notice.

### Can I pause my membership?

Yes. You can pause your membership for up to two months per year, for example while you're traveling. Let us know before your next billing date and we'll put your account on hold.

## Using the Space

### What are your opening hours?

The space is staffed Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Dedicated Desk members have 24/7 access with their key card. Day Pass and Flex members can use the space during staffed hours.

### Do I need to book a desk in advance?

Flex and Day Pass members don't need to book; desks are available on a first-come, first-served basis. On busy days (usually Tuesdays and Wednesdays), we recommend arriving early.

### Can I bring guests?

Members can bring a guest for up to two hours at no charge. Guests must sign in at the front desk. If your guest needs a desk for longer, they can purchase a Day Pass.

### Is there a quiet area?

Yes. The back room is a designated quiet zone. Please take calls in the phone booths or the lounge so others can focus.

### Are pets allowed?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome on Fridays. Please keep them on a leash in common areas and make sure they're comfortable around other people and dogs.

## Meeting Rooms

### How do I book a meeting room?

Members can book meeting rooms through the member app or at the front desk. Rooms can be booked in 30-minute blocks.

### How many meeting room hours are included?

- Flex members: 2 hours per month
- Dedicated Desk members: 5 hours per month

Additional hours can be booked at $25 per hour. Non-members can also rent rooms at $40 per hour.

### What equipment do the meeting rooms have?

All rooms have a large display with HDMI and USB-C connections, a whiteboard, and a conference speaker for video calls. The larger room seats up to 10 people.

## Mail and Address Services

### Can I use the space as my business address?

Yes. Dedicated Desk members can use our address as their registered business address at no extra cost. Flex members can add this for $15 per month.

### Do you accept packages?

We accept mail and packages for members during staffed hours. We'll notify you by email when something arrives. We can't accept packages that require refrigeration or are larger than 50 lbs.

## Billing

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards. Memberships are billed automatically on the first of each month.

### How do I cancel my membership?

Send us an email or let the front desk know at least 30 days before your next billing date. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.
