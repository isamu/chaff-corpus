# FAQ

## How does Pantry Box work?

Each week, you choose your meals from our menu, and we deliver a box with pre-measured ingredients and step-by-step recipe cards. Most recipes take 30 to 40 minutes to cook.

## Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver in the Denver metro area and Boulder. Enter your ZIP code at checkout to check whether we deliver to your address.

## When will my box arrive?

Boxes are delivered on Sundays or Wednesdays, depending on your area. You'll see your delivery day before you place your first order, and we'll send you a text on the morning of delivery with a time window.

## Do I need to be home for delivery?

No. Your box is packed with ice packs and insulated liners that keep the ingredients cold for up to 8 hours after delivery. If you won't be home, you can leave delivery instructions in your account (for example, "leave at the side door").

## How do I choose my meals?

You can pick your meals in your account up until 11:59 p.m. on Wednesday for the following week's delivery. If you don't choose, we'll pick meals for you based on your preferences.

## Can I skip a week?

Yes. You can skip any week in your account, as long as you do it before the Wednesday 11:59 p.m. cutoff. There's no fee for skipping, and you can skip as many weeks as you need.

## How do I cancel?

You can cancel your subscription anytime from your account settings. Cancellations made before the Wednesday cutoff apply to the next week's box. There are no cancellation fees.

## Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Every week, at least four of our meals are vegetarian. You can set "vegetarian" as a preference in your account so those meals show up first.

## What about allergies?

Each recipe lists its allergens, including dairy, eggs, wheat, soy, tree nuts, peanuts, fish and shellfish. However, all of our meals are packed in the same facility, so we can't guarantee that any meal is free from cross-contact. If you have a severe allergy, we don't recommend our service.

## What do I do with the packaging?

The cardboard box and paper liners can go in your curbside recycling. The ice packs are filled with non-toxic gel: you can cut them open, empty the gel into the trash, and recycle the plastic film where accepted. You can also leave your empty box out on your next delivery day and our driver will take it back for reuse.

## Something was missing or damaged in my box. What should I do?

Sorry about that! Contact us within 48 hours of delivery through the Help page in your account, and let us know what was missing or damaged. We'll credit your account for the affected items.

## How can I contact you?

Email us at support@pantrybox.example or use the chat in your account. Our team is available Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Mountain Time.
