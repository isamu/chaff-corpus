# Setting Up Your Work Laptop

Welcome to Fernwood! Your laptop arrives with the basics already installed, but there are a few things you need to do on your first morning before you can get to work. Plan for about an hour. If you get stuck at any point, message the IT team in the #it-help channel (you'll have access once step 3 is done) or call the IT desk at extension 4400.

## What you'll need

- Your laptop and charger
- Your phone, for two-factor authentication
- The welcome email from IT, which has your username and a temporary password

## 1. Sign in for the first time

Turn on the laptop and connect to the **Fernwood-Staff** Wi-Fi network. If you're working from home, use your own network.

Sign in with the username and temporary password from your welcome email. You'll be asked to choose a new password right away. It needs to be at least 14 characters long. A short sentence you'll remember is easier than a random string, and just as secure.

## 2. Set up two-factor authentication

1. Install the **Authenticator** app on your phone from the App Store or Google Play.
2. On your laptop, open a browser and go to **myaccount.fernwood.example**.
3. Sign in, then follow the prompts to scan the QR code with the Authenticator app.
4. Enter the six-digit code the app shows to finish.

You'll be asked for a code from the app when you sign in to email, chat, and most other work tools for the first time on a new device.

## 3. Open email and chat

Email and chat are already installed. Open each one and sign in with your new password. In chat, join **#general**, **#it-help**, and your team's channel. Your manager will tell you which other channels to join.

## 4. Let the laptop update

The laptop will start installing updates in the background once you're signed in. It may ask you to restart. Please do it when asked, even if it's inconvenient; some tools won't work until the updates are done.

You can check what's still installing in the **Company Portal** app. Anything marked "Required" will install on its own.

## 5. Install what your team uses

Open **Company Portal** and look through the list of available apps. Install anything your team uses that isn't already on the laptop. Please don't install software from other sources. If something you need isn't in Company Portal, ask in #it-help and we'll check whether it can be added.

## 6. Set up a printer (office only)

In the office, go to **Settings > Printers** and add **Fernwood-Print**. Print jobs wait in the queue until you tap your badge on any printer, so you can collect them from whichever one is closest.

## 7. Back up your work

Save your files in your **OneDrive - Fernwood** folder, not on the desktop or in Downloads. Files there are backed up automatically and you can reach them from any device. Files saved anywhere else on the laptop are not backed up, and we can't recover them if the laptop is lost or replaced.

## Looking after your laptop

- Lock the screen whenever you step away (Windows key + L).
- Don't leave the laptop in a car, even out of sight.
- If it's lost or stolen, tell IT straight away so we can lock it remotely. You won't be in trouble for reporting it.

That's it. If anything above didn't work the way it's described, let us know in #it-help so we can fix the guide for the next person.
