# Shipping Label Printer: How to Use It

This guide covers the label printer at the packing station. Use it for every outgoing order.

## Before you start

- The printer is the white Rollo-style thermal printer on the left side of the packing table. It is connected to the packing station PC by USB.
- It uses 4 × 6 inch thermal labels. Spare rolls are in the second drawer under the table.
- You don't need ink or toner. If the labels come out blank, the roll is in upside down (see "Loading a new roll" below).

## Printing a label

1. Open the order in ShipDesk on the packing station PC.
2. Check that the weight and box size on screen match the package. If you changed boxes, update the size before printing.
3. Click **Buy & Print Label**.
4. In the print window, make sure the printer is set to **Label Printer** and the paper size is **4 × 6**. These are the defaults, so you normally don't need to change anything.
5. Click **Print**. The label prints in a few seconds.
6. Tear the label off against the serrated edge, pulling down and toward you.
7. Stick the label flat on the largest side of the box. Don't put it over a seam or wrap it around an edge, or the barcode may not scan.

## Reprinting a label

If a label prints badly or gets damaged, don't buy a new one. In ShipDesk, open the order, go to **Shipments**, and click **Reprint**. Buying a second label charges us twice.

## Loading a new roll

1. Open the lid by pressing the button on the top of the printer.
2. Remove the empty core.
3. Put the new roll in so the labels come off the **bottom** of the roll, with the shiny side facing up.
4. Feed the end of the labels through the slot and close the lid.
5. The printer will feed one blank label to line itself up. Throw that label away.

## Common problems

**The printer is printing blank labels.**
The roll is upside down. Turn it over so the shiny side faces up.

**The label is printing across two labels.**
The printer has lost its position. Hold the button on top of the printer until it beeps once, then let go. It will feed a few labels and recalibrate.

**Nothing happens when I click Print.**
Check that the printer's light is green and the USB cable is plugged in at both ends. If the light is red, open and close the lid. If it still doesn't print, restart the packing station PC.

**The label prints too small, with lots of white space.**
The paper size in the print window is wrong. Change it to 4 × 6 and print again.

## Who to ask

If the printer still isn't working, tell the shift lead. Don't try to take the printer apart.
