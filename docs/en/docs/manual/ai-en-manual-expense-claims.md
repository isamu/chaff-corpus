# Expense Reimbursement Procedure

## 1. Purpose

This procedure explains how employees submit business expenses for reimbursement, what documentation is required, and how claims are reviewed and paid.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all employees who incur expenses on behalf of the company, including travel, meals with clients, training, and small purchases.

## 3. What Can Be Claimed

You can claim reasonable expenses that are necessary for your work and that were not paid for with a company card. Common examples include:

- Travel (airfare, train tickets, mileage for use of a personal car, parking, taxis or rideshares)
- Hotel accommodation while traveling on company business
- Meals while traveling, or meals with clients or partners
- Registration fees for approved training or conferences
- Office supplies or small equipment bought for work, up to $100 per item

The following are **not** reimbursable:

- Personal entertainment, such as in-room movies or minibar items
- Traffic or parking fines
- Alcohol, unless it is part of an approved client meal
- Upgrades to business or first class without prior approval
- Commuting between your home and your regular workplace

## 4. Before You Spend

- **Travel** must be approved by your manager in advance through the travel request form.
- **Purchases over $100** require your manager's approval before you buy.
- **Training and conferences** must be approved by your manager and HR.

If you are unsure whether an expense is reimbursable, ask your manager or the Finance team before you spend the money.

## 5. How to Submit a Claim

1. Log in to the expense system and select **New Expense Report**.
2. Add each expense as a separate line, with the date, amount, category, and business purpose.
3. Attach a receipt for each expense. A photo or scan is acceptable as long as it is legible.
4. For mileage, enter the start and end locations and the number of miles. The system calculates the amount at the current IRS rate.
5. Review the report and click **Submit**. It will be sent to your manager for approval.

Submit your claim within **30 days** of the date of the expense. Claims submitted after 60 days may not be paid.

## 6. Receipts

A valid receipt shows:

- The name of the vendor
- The date of purchase
- The items or services purchased
- The amount paid and the payment method

Credit card statements alone are not accepted as receipts. If you lose a receipt, complete the Missing Receipt form and attach it to your claim. Missing receipt forms are accepted for expenses under $50 and no more than twice per year.

## 7. Meals

Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to the following daily limits:

| Meal | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Breakfast | $15 |
| Lunch | $20 |
| Dinner | $40 |

For client meals, list the names and companies of everyone who attended in the business purpose field.

## 8. Approval and Payment

1. Your manager reviews the report and approves it, rejects it, or sends it back with questions.
2. Finance checks the approved report against this procedure.
3. Approved claims are paid by direct deposit with the next payroll run after Finance approval.

If a claim is rejected, you will receive an email explaining why. You can correct the report and resubmit it.

## 9. Responsibilities

- **Employees** submit accurate claims on time with the required receipts.
- **Managers** review claims within 5 business days and make sure expenses are reasonable and necessary.
- **Finance** checks claims for compliance, processes payments, and keeps records.
